\begin{abstract}
\todo{150--200 words. Structure: (1) GUI agents must ground language intents
to screen coordinates; errors are localization failures, a core
grounded-perception problem. (2) We test whether factoring the policy into
WHAT (action-type classification) and WHERE (action-type-conditioned
grounding) improves grounding in a 2B-parameter VLM (Qwen2-VL-2B + LoRA).
(3) Findings: action-type supervision helps when type is spatially
predictive; the literal learned action-embedding mechanism is NOT the winning
mechanism (auxiliary loss captures the benefit more simply); and a
cautionary mechanistic result: naive inputs\_embeds injection silently
bypasses M-RoPE and produces false negatives. (4) Controlled multi-seed,
low-data study relevant to practitioners deploying small VLMs.}
\end{abstract}
